## Overview

Poster presentation at the **1st European Cancer Dependency Map Symposium** hosted by
[Human Technopole](https://humantechnopole.it/en/) (Milan, 8 May 2023). The symposium brought together the European community working on chemo-genomics and genome-editing screens for the discovery of new cancer vulnerabilities and therapeutic targets, with talks from DepMap (Broad), the Wellcome Sanger Institute, EMBL-EBI, and other groups leading the field.

- **Symposium:** [1st European Cancer Dependency Map Symposium](https://humantechnopole.it/en/trainings/1st-european-cancer-dependency-map-symposium/)
- **Venue:** Human Technopole, Milan, Italy
- **Date:** 8 May 2023
- **Format:** poster presentation
